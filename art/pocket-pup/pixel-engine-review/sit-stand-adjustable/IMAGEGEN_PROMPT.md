Flat warm cream background, no labels, no borders, no props, no arrows, no extras. Pixel edges crisp with an apparent 64x64 native grid for each dog, integer-scale enlarged. Tail curl follows the pelvis late with a clean fixed join, no dark double line, pinholes or transparent rows through the body. Leave the golden flank mostly plain, no diagonal interior strokes, checkerboard shading or thick belly marks. Make hip positions descend progressively; hold coherent chest/paw lengths so frames 2–7 read as one dog in a continuous action. Prioritize clean silhouettes, consistent volume and distinct readable poses.
