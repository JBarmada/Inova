# What We Want the App to Become

Vision backlog. These ideas describe the whole product ambition, including features beyond the first implementation.

# A game people care about

- Adopt a pixel dog from a small selection.
- Keep the dog as a movable desktop companion.
- Give it expressive happy, sad, excited, tired, and playful reactions.
- Customize it with mix-and-match clothes, accessories, and props earned through experiences.
- Unlock treats, toys, and ways to interact with it.
- Expand its world as the user explores and grows.
- Make interactions feel alive through animation, sound, effects, and memorable rewards.

# A companion that helps the user grow

- Understand commitments through manual input and, where feasible, permitted integrations.
- Break responsibilities into manageable quests and suggest useful times to do them.
- Offer encouragement adapted to the user's preferences.
- Support journaling and remember information the user chooses to save.
- Recall relevant past experiences and things the user previously told themselves.
- Offer practical adulting help when requested, including shopping, routines, and conflict conversations.
- Suggest occasional exploration beyond the user's current interests or skills.
- Offer optional perspective exercises, different viewpoints, and discussion that helps users examine their own reasoning.

# An expanding personal world

A bookshelf holds dated, readable memories. Other places might grow out of the user's experiences, such as a library for learning or new spaces for exploration. The world should feel personal through the experiences it represents.

# Possible sources of context

- Calendar events and assignment deadlines.
- Academic progress or grades from Brightspace.
- User-written reflections and optional photo submissions.
- Sleep information.
- App activity.

Every integration remains subject to access, platform, and effort checks. Each source needs a specific user benefit and optional participation. Planned events and photos provide limited context, rather than proof of a person's overall wellbeing.

# Comical transformations

Possible secrets include an eye-bag dog after a reported all-nighter, zombie or skeleton appearances, a revival potion, and a lightning revival. The mode and triggering rules are open. Preserve these as design ideas rather than silently committing them to default behavior.
